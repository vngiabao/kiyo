# Tingjian 听见

A personal, conversation-first Mandarin study application for adult beginners, initially English speaking. The name is a working product name chosen for this build.

## Product truth
The user's two supplied briefs are the source of requirements. Success means understanding and producing useful Mandarin in unfamiliar contexts. Oral and written capabilities remain independent. No fabricated assessment, native review, tone scoring, or exam predictions.

## First release
An offline-capable local application with onboarding, structured foundation lessons, listening-first exercises, local recording, contextual scripted conversation, FSRS per-modality review, an initial media practice loop, dictionary, evidence-based progress and portable learner records. Advanced curriculum, professionally recorded media, validated pronunciation assessment, cloud sync, full Vietnamese instruction and formal exam readiness are expansion work, not claims of this release.

## Use scene and design commitments
Daily, focused adult study at home or on a phone. Calm private study room with the user-approved Oura-inspired night palette: midnight navy surfaces, soft white text, pale blue actions, quiet teal success, and warm peach errors. The approved slender, small-face Sayo provides restrained decorative company. Preserve the established editorial typography and layout. Chinese leads; pinyin is revealable support. Today answers what to do next. Focused study removes navigation. Purpose-built listening, conversation and media surfaces instead of equal card grids.

## Decisions from the explicit brief
Proceed autonomously; do not interrupt for trivial implementation choices. No further design interview is needed because the user supplied the audience, goals, workflow, aesthetic, quality bar and technical priorities. Browser-first local persistence is the initial deployment assumption. Instructional content must retain review status.
