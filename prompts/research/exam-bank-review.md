# Original rotating HSK practice banks

The owner approved approximately 60 checkpoint and 30 sample items for each of levels 1–3. These are original, AI-assisted practice items, with native review pending. They are not official papers or an HSK score predictor.

## Format evidence

Primary sources: [official syllabus](https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus), [official 3.0 sample archive](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip). Local research copies are outside app assets in `.cache/hsk3-sample-pdfs/`.

The inspected sample papers have listening and reading in levels 1–3 and writing in levels 2–3. The inspected level-3 writing section asks for character completion from pinyin and production from a picture and supplied word. Kiyo's multiple-choice word ordering is an adaptation for grammar practice; it is not represented as that writing section. A short 12-item Kiyo timed selection does not reproduce the full paper's length or timing. GF0025-2021 teaching coverage is audited separately from the newer exam syllabus.

## Level 1 editorial review

- Preserve every ID, choice and answer in the original 36-item version-1 pack. That pack contains 24 checkpoint items and 12 sample items. Add 54 new items: 36 checkpoint and 18 sample, giving 60/30 overall.
- New items interleave reading comprehension, listening comprehension and ordinary word-order selection. Questions, options, connected sentences and ordering segments must pass the dictionary-backed level and pinyin check. No invented word levels.
- Removed out-of-level/unlisted draft vocabulary, including English/coffee/apple/classroom/subway forms; replaced them with validated level-1 contexts. Corrected a malformed ordering distractor and required every ordering choice to have the same Han-character multiset as the answer. All four answer positions appear.
- Checked identity versus occupation, departure versus arrival, day versus time, item price versus total, explicit negatives and source-specific quantities. Each key has an explanation tied to the stated sentence. An alternative cannot be correct just because it is generally plausible.
- Topic-first/marked Chinese orders can be grammatical. Ordering tasks explicitly ask for the ordinary course order that answers the given question. These items still need native review for naturalness and distractor ambiguity; they are never marked natively reviewed.
- New history counts actual displayed questions. Legacy records retain their original IDs and historical selections. New runs must use unseen items first, then least-seen/oldest exposure, without mutating completed records. Tests cover selection, sticky help, backup restore, cross-level rejection and retained legacy grades.
- A listening prompt hides its transcript until help is revealed, and revealing words/pinyin permanently marks that answer supported. A timed selection offers no reveal. Audio uses the approved Sayo identity; exact failed takes follow the owner's dated repair-and-deferral policy.

## Pending native review

All new items remain AI-assisted drafts.
