# Original rotating HSK practice banks

The owner approved approximately 60 checkpoint and 30 sample items for each of levels 1–3. These are original, AI-assisted practice items, with native review pending. They are not official papers or an HSK score predictor.

## Format evidence

Primary sources: [official syllabus](https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus), [official 3.0 sample archive](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip). Local research copies are outside app assets in `.cache/hsk3-sample-pdfs/`.

The inspected sample papers have listening and reading in levels 1–3 and writing in levels 2–3. The inspected level-3 writing section asks for character completion from pinyin and production from a picture and supplied word. Kiyo's multiple-choice word ordering is an adaptation for grammar practice; it is not represented as that writing section. A short 12-item Kiyo timed selection does not reproduce the full paper's length or timing. GF0025-2021 teaching coverage is audited separately from the newer exam syllabus.
